# Complete Semester and Subject Breakdown

## Semester 1 | Engineering Foundation Courses
Semester 1 of the CSBS program at BMSCE covers foundational engineering and science subjects. Mathematics I (MA1) introduces differential calculus, integral calculus, linear algebra including eigenvalues and eigenvectors, and vector calculus including gradient, divergence, curl, line integrals, surface integrals, Green theorem, Stokes theorem, and Gauss divergence theorem. Engineering Physics (PHY1) covers modern physics, quantum mechanics, laser physics, fiber optics, and semiconductor physics with complete derivations and numerical problems. Elements of Civil Engineering (CIV1) introduces surveying, building materials, and structural concepts. Elements of Mechanical Engineering (ME1) covers energy sources, thermodynamics basics, and mechanical systems. Basic Electrical Engineering (EE1) covers DC and AC circuits, Kirchhoff laws, network theorems, transformers, and electrical machines. Laboratory courses include Engineering Physics Lab (PHY1L) and Civil Engineering Lab (CIV1L).

## Semester 2 | Programming and Engineering Sciences
Mathematics II (MA2) covers ordinary differential equations, Laplace transforms, Fourier series, partial differential equations, and numerical methods including Newton Raphson, interpolation, numerical integration, and Runge Kutta methods. Engineering Chemistry (CHE2) covers electrochemistry, polymer chemistry, water chemistry, corrosion, and instrumental analysis methods. Problem Solving with C Programming (PSP) is the first programming course covering data types, operators, control structures, arrays, strings, functions, pointers, structures, unions, file handling, and dynamic memory allocation. Basic Electronics (EE2) covers semiconductor devices, transistors, operational amplifiers, and digital electronics basics. Constitution of India and Professional Ethics (CCP) covers constitutional law and engineering ethics. Lab courses include C Programming Lab (PSPL) and Chemistry Lab (CHE2L).

## Semester 3 | Core Computer Science Foundation
Mathematics III (MA3) covers statistics and probability distributions (binomial, Poisson, normal, exponential), correlation, regression, curve fitting, Z transforms, and complex analysis. Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA) is a critical subject covering arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees (binary, BST, AVL, B trees), graphs (BFS, DFS, shortest paths, MST), hashing, and all major sorting and searching algorithms with time complexity analysis. Object Oriented Programming with C++ (OOP) covers classes, inheritance, polymorphism, operator overloading, virtual functions, templates, exception handling, and STL. Digital Electronics (DE) covers Boolean algebra, logic gates, combinational circuits, sequential circuits, flip flops, registers, and counters. Unix Programming (UNIX) covers shell commands, file systems, shell scripting, and system calls. Lab courses include DSA Lab (DSAL) and OOP Lab (OOPL).

## Semester 4 | Systems, Databases, and Business
Mathematics IV (MA4) covers complex analysis, contour integration, residue theorem, hypothesis testing, chi square test, t test, F test, and ANOVA. Database Management Systems (DBMS) covers relational model, ER diagrams, SQL, normalization (1NF through BCNF), transaction management, ACID properties, concurrency control, indexing, and NoSQL databases. Computer Organization and Architecture (COA) covers CPU design, ALU, control unit, pipelining, memory hierarchy, cache memory, virtual memory, and I/O organization. Operating Systems (OS) covers process management, CPU scheduling algorithms, process synchronization, deadlock handling, memory management, paging, segmentation, virtual memory, page replacement algorithms, file systems, and disk scheduling. Financial Accounting and Business Statistics (FABS) covers accounting principles, financial statements, ratio analysis, cost accounting, and business statistics. Lab courses include DBMS Lab (DBMSL) and OS Lab (OSL).

## Semester 5 | Networks, AI, and Web Technologies
Computer Networks (CN) covers OSI model, TCP/IP suite, physical layer encoding, data link layer protocols, IP addressing and subnetting, routing algorithms (RIP, OSPF, BGP), TCP and UDP transport protocols, congestion control, and application layer protocols (HTTP, DNS, SMTP, FTP). Software Engineering (SE) covers software process models, requirements engineering, UML diagrams, software design patterns, testing methodologies, project management, and COCOMO estimation. Artificial Intelligence (AI) covers intelligent agents, search algorithms (BFS, DFS, A star), game playing (minimax, alpha beta pruning), knowledge representation, planning, Bayesian networks, and expert systems. Web Technologies (WEB) covers HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, responsive design, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, and REST APIs. Business Analytics (BA) covers data visualization, descriptive and predictive analytics, regression, time series analysis, and data mining. Lab courses include CN Lab (CNL) and Web Technologies Lab (WEBL).

## Semester 6 | Machine Learning and Advanced Computing
Machine Learning (ML) covers supervised learning algorithms (linear and logistic regression, decision trees, random forests, SVM, KNN, naive Bayes), unsupervised learning (K means, hierarchical clustering, DBSCAN, PCA), neural networks and deep learning basics (CNN, RNN), ensemble methods (bagging, boosting), and model evaluation metrics. Cloud Computing (CC) covers cloud models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS), virtualization, containerization with Docker, cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP), serverless computing, and microservices. Information Security (IS) covers symmetric and asymmetric encryption (DES, AES, RSA), hash functions, digital signatures, network security, firewalls, intrusion detection, web security, and cyber security frameworks. Organizational Behavior (OB) covers individual and group behavior, motivation theories, leadership styles, organizational culture, and change management. Lab courses include ML Lab (MLL).

## Semester 7 | Emerging Technologies and Projects
Big Data Analytics (BD) covers Hadoop ecosystem (HDFS, MapReduce, YARN), Apache Spark, NoSQL databases, data warehousing, and real time analytics. Internet of Things (IOT) covers IoT architecture, sensors, communication protocols (MQTT, CoAP, Zigbee), IoT platforms, edge computing, and IoT applications. Professional Elective 1 (PE1) and Open Elective 1 (OE1) provide specialization options. Project Phase 1 (PRJ) involves major project proposal and initial implementation.

## Semester 8 | Final Year Completion
Professional Elective 2 (PE2) and Professional Elective 3 (PE3) offer advanced specializations. Project Phase 2 (PRJF) involves complete project implementation, documentation, and presentation. Internship (INT) provides industry experience with documentation requirements. Seminar (SEM) involves technical presentation on emerging technologies.
